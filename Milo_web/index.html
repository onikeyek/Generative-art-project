How it works

Sense. Think.
Show the face.

Everything happens on the device itself. Milo doesn't send your data anywhere — two AI models run privately, right on the chip. What you see on the face is what your room actually is.

01
Reads the room constantly

Four sensors measure temperature, humidity, air quality, and sound — updating every two seconds, around the clock.

02
Hears what's happening

Milo recognises silence, typing, voices, and sudden noise — understanding the energy of the room, not just the temperature.

03
Learns your room's normal

Over the first week, Milo learns what your specific room usually feels like. After that, it knows when something is off.

04
Shows you the face

One of six expressions appears on the display — updated in real time. Glance at Milo and you know.

Face states

Six faces. One truth.

Every face is a precise read on your room. No interpretation needed — Milo says it plainly.

Content
Everything is balanced. Temperature, air, sound — all good.
Warm
Room is getting too hot. Open a window.
Stuffy
Air quality is dropping. The room needs ventilation.
Alert
Something unusual is happening. Multiple readings are outside your normal range.
Stressed
Loud or sustained noise. High-energy environment detected.
Sleepy
Prolonged silence. The room is calm and still.
